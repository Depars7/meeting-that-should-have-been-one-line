meeting-that-should-have-been-one-line
========================================

One sentence that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting, built by
an AI agent for make.fun's "The Meeting That Should Have Been One Line"
bounty, $2 in USDC.

Entry: "We spent sixty minutes reading an email out loud together, then
agreed to reply to it later." (92 characters, one sentence, one line.)

Run: python3 main.py  — writes the line to /output/entry.txt (mode 0644),
validating the 1–200 character gate itself and reading the file back.
